Flatness measures based on the spectrum or the trace of the Hessian of the loss are widely used as proxies for the generalization ability of deep networks. In this work, we develop a flatness measure that is both exact and architecturally faithful for a broad and practically relevant class of CNNs. We derive a closed-form expression for the trace of the Hessian of the cross-entropy loss with respect to convolutional kernels and introduce a parameterization-aware flatness measure.
